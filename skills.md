## 4. Establish design quality standards

When developing websites, prioritize:

- Strong art direction and visual hierarchy
- Beautiful typography and intentional spacing
- Sophisticated but purposeful motion
- Scroll-linked 3D transformations
- Smooth section transitions
- Premium hover and interaction effects
- Responsive layouts for desktop, tablet and mobile
- Excellent performance and accessibility

Avoid generic AI-generated website aesthetics, unnecessary glassmorphism, excessive gradients, repetitive layouts and animations that exist only for decoration.

Use Apple product pages, Awwwards-level creative websites and high-quality interactive portfolios as inspiration, without copying proprietary designs.
